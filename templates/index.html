💡 How to Connect Your Smartphone (DroidCam):
  1. Download the free DroidCam app on your phone (Android/iOS).
  2. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi router as this GameWatch PC.
  3. Open DroidCam, note the Wi-Fi IP & Port (4747), enter: http://<phone_ip>:4747/video.
❓ Feed not appearing or showing offline? (Troubleshooting) ▼
  • Check Same Wi-Fi: Phone and PC must be on the same local Wi-Fi router subnet (not mobile cellular data).
  • Test in Browser Tab: Paste the stream URL (e.g. http://192.168.1.105:8080/video) into a new Chrome tab. If you see video there, GameWatch will connect instantly!
  • Windows Firewall: Ensure Python is allowed through Windows Defender Firewall for Private Networks.
  • Keep App in Foreground: Do not lock your phone screen or let the camera app sleep in the background.
